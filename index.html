Everything it does.

Every feature below is in the app today — and every one of them runs on your PC.

Speak naturally

Talk the way you talk.

Fillers disappear
“Um”, “uh”, “eee” and stutters are removed — by simple rules, without an extra AI model.
Voice commands
“New line”, “new paragraph”, “delete that”.
Say “send”
End a dictation with “send” and Enter is pressed for you — per app, if you like.
Snippets
“My email” becomes your address; any phrase can expand to any text.
Numbers as digits
Spoken Turkish numbers are written as digits: “on beş” → 15.
Never lose a word

Nothing slips through.

First word never clipped
The microphone is ready before you press, and keeps the half second before it.
Keep talking
Start the next dictation while the last one is still being written — it is queued, not lost.
Lands where you started
Switch apps or tabs and the text still goes to the right box, or waits until you are back.
Long thoughts, short waits
Long dictations are transcribed in pieces while you are still speaking.
Paste it again
Shift + Alt + Z types the last dictation once more; Shift + Alt + X copies it.
Learns you

It gets your words right.

Your vocabulary
Names, products and jargon are spelled the way you write them.
Learns from corrections
Fix a word after dictating and FlowLocal remembers it next time.
Per-app profiles
A different language, digits or “send” for each app.
24 languages
Pick a main language; engines trained for a single language are one click away.
Your stats
Words dictated this week and the time it saved you over typing.
Respects your PC

Light on your machine.

Frees the GPU when idle
The model leaves graphics memory after a quiet stretch and comes back when you talk.
Steps aside for games
Ctrl + Win + Alt hides it completely; full-screen games are left alone.
Never types passwords
Password fields are skipped — nothing is typed or copied there.
No phantom text
The “thanks for watching” lines speech models invent in silence are filtered out.
Picks itself up
If it ever crashes, Windows restarts it. Works in terminals too.
